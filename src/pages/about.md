# About this site

Redeemed exists for one reason: to set out the good news of Jesus Christ plainly, for anyone who is searching, and to point them to the Scriptures and to a faithful local church.

## What we believe

This site holds to the **Westminster Confession of Faith** and the **Larger and Shorter Catechisms** as faithful summaries of the teaching of Holy Scripture, the only infallible rule of faith and life.

In particular:

- **Scripture alone.** The Bible is the inspired, inerrant Word of God (WCF 1).
- **God's sovereign grace.** Salvation is entirely God's work, from first to last (WCF 3, 10).
- **The free offer of the gospel.** Christ is freely and sincerely offered to all who hear, and all are called to repent and believe (WCF 7.3; Shorter Catechism 86).
- **One covenant of grace.** There is one people of God and one way of salvation, through Christ, in both Old and New Testaments (WCF 7).
- **Justification by faith alone.** We are declared righteous by God on the basis of Christ's righteousness alone, received through faith alone (WCF 11).
- **The church and the means of grace.** Christ ordinarily gathers and grows his people through the Word, sacraments, and prayer, in the life of the local church (WCF 25; Shorter Catechism 88).

## What this site is not

It is not a church, and it is not a substitute for one. Please [find a congregation](/church/) where Christ is preached, and speak with a minister there.

## Privacy

This site uses **Google Analytics** to count visits and see which pages are read, so we can tell whether the site is helping anyone. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and collects information such as the pages you visit, roughly where you are (city or region), and the kind of device and browser you use. We do not ask for your name or email, and we do not try to identify you.

The data is processed by Google under its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can block it with your browser's privacy settings, a content blocker, or Google's [opt-out add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).
