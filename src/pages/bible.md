# Hear God speak for himself

The Bible is God's Word. It is not merely a book about God; it is God speaking. Through it, by his Spirit, he makes himself and his salvation known. You don't need to be an expert to begin. Read slowly, read honestly, and ask God to help you understand.

> All Scripture is breathed out by God and profitable for teaching, for reproof, for correction, and for training in righteousness,
> <cite>2 Timothy 3:16</cite>

## Where to start

- **The Gospel of John.** It was written "so that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that by believing you may have life in his name" (John 20:31).
- **Romans 1–8.** The clearest explanation in Scripture of sin, grace, and how God makes sinners right with himself.
- **The Psalms.** The prayers and songs of God's people, for every season of life.

You can read the ESV for free at [esv.org](https://www.esv.org), or ask any of the churches on our [church page](/church/) for a Bible. Most will gladly give you one.

## A 30-day plan

One chapter a day. If you miss a day, just pick up where you left off.

<div class="table-scroll">

| Days | Read | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–21 | John 1–21, one chapter a day | Who does Jesus say he is? What does he come to do? |
| 22–29 | Romans 1–8, one chapter a day | Why do we need saving, and how does God save? |
| 30 | Psalm 51 | A sinner's prayer for mercy, in God's own words |

</div>

## A few questions to ask as you read

1. What does this passage show me about God?
2. What does it show me about myself?
3. What does it show me about Jesus Christ?
4. How should I respond?

The Bible is best read not only alone but also with God's people, under faithful preaching. When you're ready, [find a church](/church/) where the Bible is taught.
