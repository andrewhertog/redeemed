# For further reading

A short, careful list. It is not meant to be exhaustive. If you read only one thing, read the Bible: start with [the Gospel of John](/bible/).

<h2 id="standards">The Westminster Standards</h2>

The confession and catechisms that Presbyterian churches hold as their summary of what the Bible teaches. They are freely available online from the Orthodox Presbyterian Church.

- [The Westminster Confession of Faith](https://opc.org/wcf.html): thirty-three chapters covering the whole of Christian doctrine.
- [The Westminster Shorter Catechism](https://opc.org/sc.html): 107 questions and answers. Begin here.
- [The Westminster Larger Catechism](https://opc.org/lc.html): a fuller treatment, especially of the Christian life.

### Help understanding them

- **G. I. Williamson**, *The Shorter Catechism for Study Classes*
- **G. I. Williamson**, *The Westminster Confession of Faith for Study Classes*
- **Thomas Watson**, *A Body of Divinity*: a Puritan's warm-hearted exposition of the Shorter Catechism
- **Chad Van Dixhoorn**, *Confessing the Faith*: a chapter-by-chapter reader's guide to the Confession
- **R. C. Sproul**, *Truths We Confess*: an accessible commentary on the whole Confession

<h2 id="three-forms">The Three Forms of Unity</h2>

The confessions of the Reformed churches listed on our [church page](/church/). They teach the same gospel as the Westminster Standards.

- [The Belgic Confession](https://canrc.org/the-belgic-confession): thirty-seven articles summarizing the Reformed faith.
- [The Heidelberg Catechism](https://canrc.org/heidelberg-catechism): 129 questions and answers, beginning with "What is your only comfort in life and death?"
- [The Canons of Dort](https://canrc.org/canons-of-dort): on God's sovereign grace in salvation.

## Books for those asking questions

Clear, faithful introductions to the Christian faith. Most are by Presbyterian authors; a few are classics from the wider Reformed tradition that have helped generations of seekers.

- **Greg Gilbert**, *What Is the Gospel?*: a short, clear statement of the message on the [home page](/).
- **R. C. Sproul**, *The Holiness of God*: who God is, and why it matters that he is holy.
- **J. I. Packer**, *Knowing God*: a modern classic on the character of God.
- **Michael Horton**, *Core Christianity*: the main teachings of the Bible for newcomers.
- **Sinclair Ferguson**, *The Christian Life: A Doctrinal Introduction*: what it means to belong to Christ.
- **C. H. Spurgeon**, *All of Grace*: an earnest, tender plea to come to Christ.
- **John Bunyan**, *The Pilgrim's Progress*: the Christian life as a journey, told as a story.
